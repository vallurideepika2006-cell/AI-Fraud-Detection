Understanding the Metrics

Accuracy

The percentage of all test transactions classified correctly. Accuracy alone can be misleading when fraud is rare.

Precision

Of the transactions predicted as fraud, how many were actually fraudulent? Higher precision means fewer false alarms.

Recall

Of all actual fraudulent transactions, how many did the model identify? Low recall means more fraud cases are missed.

F1-score

The harmonic mean of precision and recall. It helps compare the balance between detecting fraud and avoiding false alarms.

Important Note

Credit card fraud datasets are highly imbalanced: legitimate transactions greatly outnumber fraudulent ones. Evaluate models using precision, recall, F1-score, and the confusion matrix. These metrics are for an academic prototype and do not guarantee real-world fraud detection performance.
